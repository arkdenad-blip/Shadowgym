Our Story

Established in 1999 in Akropong Akuapem, Shadow Gym began as a small personal space for friends. Today, it is the home of champions.

We have hosted Akuapem Strongest and produced legends like Kofi Gyiwa, Kobby, Skylover, Dennis, and Kasay.
